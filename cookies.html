In short

  • We use only essential storage in your browser: what the app needs to work and to keep you logged in.
  • No advertising cookies, no tracking, no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel.
  • Because everything we store is essential, there is nothing to switch off. We show a one-time notice so you know.
  • Public PG pages store nothing in your browser. Visits are counted on our side without cookies.

2. Other services the pages load

  • Google (fonts.gstatic.com, www.gstatic.com): letter shapes and the app's drawing engine. Like any website, Google can see your device's internet address when the file is fetched. We send no personal data and Google sets no cookies through our app.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs: a small QR-code tool on guest pages, checked with a security fingerprint so it can't be changed.
  • Supabase: our database and login service (India, Mumbai region).

3. How to remove it

  • Log out in the app (Settings → Log out), or use "Log out of all devices".
  • Or clear this website's data in your browser settings. You will need to log in again.

4. If this changes

If we ever add anything that is not essential (for example, marketing tools), we will ask for your permission first and you can say no.
