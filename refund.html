Refund & Cancellation Policy

In short

  • No hidden fees. The price in My plan & payment is the full price. If tax (GST) ever applies, it is shown before you pay.
  • No automatic charges. We never take money from your account. You pay each month or year yourself.
  • New owners: full refund if you ask within 7 days of your first-ever payment.
  • Stop any time: simply don't renew. Your plan keeps working until the paid-until date.

1. How you pay

  • You pay PG Manager per PG, monthly or yearly, by UPI or bank transfer to the details shown in the app (or online by card/UPI if offered to you).
  • A yearly plan costs 10 months' price: 2 months free.
  • Your plan is active once the payment is confirmed. You get a receipt in the app.
  • Special prices agreed with you are written in your plan note in the app.

2. When you get money back

SituationWhat happens
First-ever payment, asked within 7 daysFull refund, no questions asked.
Paid twice or paid the wrong amountThe extra amount is refunded in full.
PG Manager was down for more than 3 days in a row because of usYour plan is extended by those days, free.
Stopped using it during a paid month or yearNo refund for the period already started. The plan stays active until the paid-until date.
Changed to a bigger planYou pay only the new plan's price from the change; the old plan's unused days are adjusted.
Changed to a smaller planStarts from your next renewal. No refund of the difference.
Account paused or closed for breaking the TermsNo refund.

3. How to ask for a refund

  • In the app: Help & complaints → new complaint → "Payment / refund". Or email from your login email.
  • Tell us the payment date, amount and UPI reference number.
  • We reply within 3 working days. Approved refunds go back to the same UPI ID or bank account within 7 working days.

4. Rent and deposits from guests

Rent, advance and deposits that guests pay go directly to the PG owner (their UPI ID or their own Razorpay account). PG Manager never holds this money, so refunds of rent or deposits are between the guest and the PG owner. If an owner uses online rent by Razorpay, Razorpay's charges are billed by Razorpay to the owner, not by PG Manager.

5. Cancelling

  • There is nothing to cancel: plans do not renew by themselves. If you don't pay, the account gets a short grace period and is then paused.
  • Your data is kept for at least 30 days after a pause so you can renew or ask for a copy. See the Privacy Policy.
  • To close your account completely: Settings → Privacy & terms → Ask about my data → "Close my account".
