In short

  • PG Manager helps paying-guest (PG) owners manage rooms, guests, rent, meals and repairs.
  • We collect only what a PG needs to run: who stays, where, rent paid, meal choices and repairs.
  • We never sell your data, never show ads and never use it for marketing. No tracking cookies (Cookie Policy).
  • ID photos and selfies are deleted automatically a few days after a guest leaves.
  • Data is stored in India (Mumbai) and protected by access rules.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data in the app (Settings → Privacy & terms) or by email: .

1. Who is responsible for your data

is run by , . Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • If you are a guest: your PG (the owner) decides why and how your data is used. The PG is the Data Fiduciary. PG Manager is the software the PG uses and handles your data only on the PG's behalf (Data Processor).
  • If you enquire on a PG's public page: the PG you write to is the Data Fiduciary; PG Manager delivers your message to them.
  • If you are a PG owner or staff member: PG Manager is the Data Fiduciary for your own account data (your name, email, phone and plan).

2. What we collect

Guests

  • Name, phone and WhatsApp number, email, gender, food and tea/coffee preference.
  • Home address, emergency contact and parent/guardian details.
  • ID type, ID photo and a selfie (only when you add them, and only with your consent). For Aadhaar, only the masked copy; we never ask you to type an Aadhaar number.
  • Room and bed, joining, notice and leaving dates.
  • Rent, token advance, refundable deposit, payments, receipts and UPI reference numbers.
  • Meal choices and meal-counter check-ins, notices you read, and repair requests with photos.

People who enquire on a PG's public page or ask for a trial meal

  • Name, phone number, the move-in date and message you type, and your consent to be contacted. Nothing else.
  • To stop spam, a scrambled code made from your internet address (not the address itself) is kept for 2 days.
  • Page visits are counted per day and per source (for example "from WhatsApp") without cookies and without your internet address.

PG owners and staff

  • Name, email, phone, PG name and address, UPI ID, logo, plan and subscription payments.
  • Business figures you choose to record (expenses and income for "Profit per bed"), your PG's Google Business link, and the review requests you send.
  • Records of what you agreed to (version and date) and important actions such as acknowledged notifications.

Technical

  • Only essential browser storage, such as your login, so you stay signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies. See the Cookie Policy.
  • Error reports (what failed, never your guests' details) to keep the app working.
  • Wrong guest-login attempts with a device label, to stop people guessing logins.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the PG: rooms and beds, stays, rent dues, receipts, deposits and refunds.
  • To plan meals and reduce food waste, and to handle repair requests.
  • For guest safety and records the law may require (for example, sharing details with the police only when the law asks for it).
  • To send service messages such as rent reminders, login codes and receipts (by email or WhatsApp).
  • To answer owners' questions in "Ask PG Manager" (see section 5).
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and prevent misuse.

Guest data is collected with the guest's consent, given on the joining form or to the PG at check-in. Owner account data is used to provide the service the owner signed up for. We collect nothing "just in case".

5. AI answers

  • "Ask PG Manager" gives every owner a free helper that searches our help pages and the owner's own figures. Nothing leaves our system.
  • Owners on the Elite plan can also get AI answers (Anthropic, see above). We keep only a count of questions for the monthly limit, not the questions themselves.
  • AI answers can be wrong. They are suggestions for the owner; no decision about a guest is made automatically.

7. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS) and data is encrypted at rest by our database provider.
  • Database access rules let each person see only what their role allows.
  • ID photos and guest photos are stored privately, never as public links.
  • Login by one-time email code; limits on wrong attempts and on form submissions; secret keys are kept on the server, never in the app.
  • Encrypted nightly backups of the database and photos, kept for 30 days (so data deleted from the app also leaves the backups within 30 days), checked by restoring them, and monitoring for errors and misuse.

If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask to:

  • See the data held about you and who it was shared with.
  • Correct or update wrong or old details.
  • Delete your data, when it is no longer needed and the law allows.
  • Withdraw consent, as easily as you gave it. After withdrawal we stop using the data, but services that need it (for example, staying at the PG) may no longer be possible.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act.

Owners and staff: in the app, Settings → Privacy & terms → Ask about my data. You get a reference number and can see our reply there. Or email us from your login email.

Guests and people who enquired: please ask the PG first; they can correct most details in the app. You can also email us and we will pass it to the PG and help.

We reply within 30 days. If we cannot do what you ask (for example, rent records the law requires us to keep), we tell you why.

9. Children

Owner and staff accounts are only for adults (18 and over). PG Manager is meant for adult guests. If a guest is under 18, the joining form asks for a parent's or guardian's name, phone number and consent, and the PG owner must confirm with the parent or guardian before approving the guest. We do not use children's data for tracking, ads or profiling.
