In short

  • PG Manager helps paying-guest (PG) owners manage rooms, guests, rent, meals and repairs.
  • We collect only what a PG needs to run: who stays, where, rent paid, meal choices and repairs.
  • We never sell your data, never show ads and never use it for marketing.
  • ID photos and selfies are deleted automatically a few days after a guest leaves.
  • Data is stored in India (Mumbai), protected by access rules, and backed up.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data: email .

1. Who is responsible for your data

PG Manager is run by (India). Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • If you are a guest: your PG (the owner) decides why and how your data is used. The PG is the Data Fiduciary. PG Manager is the software the PG uses and handles your data only on the PG's behalf (Data Processor).
  • If you are a PG owner or staff member: PG Manager is the Data Fiduciary for your own account data (your name, email, phone and plan).

2. What we collect

Guests

  • Name, phone and WhatsApp number, email, gender, food and tea/coffee preference.
  • Home address, emergency contact and parent/guardian details.
  • ID type, ID photo and a selfie (only when you add them, and only with your consent).
  • Room and bed, joining, notice and leaving dates.
  • Rent, token advance, refundable deposit, payments, receipts and UPI reference numbers.
  • Meal choices and meal-counter check-ins, notices you read, and repair requests with photos.

PG owners and staff

  • Name, email, phone, PG name and address, UPI ID, logo, plan and subscription payments.
  • Records of what you agreed to (version and date) and important actions such as acknowledged notifications.

Technical

  • Your login is kept in your browser so you stay signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Error reports (what failed, never your guests' details) to keep the app working.
  • Wrong guest-login attempts with a device label, to stop people guessing logins.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the PG: rooms and beds, stays, rent dues, receipts, deposits and refunds.
  • To plan meals and reduce food waste, and to handle repair requests.
  • For guest safety and records the law may require (for example, sharing details with the police only when the law asks for it).
  • To send service messages such as rent reminders, login codes and receipts (by email or WhatsApp).
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and prevent misuse.

Guest data is collected with the guest's consent, given on the joining form or to the PG at check-in. Owner account data is used to provide the service the owner signed up for.

6. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS) and data is encrypted at rest by our database provider.
  • Database access rules let each person see only what their role allows.
  • ID photos and guest photos are stored privately, never as public links.
  • Login by one-time email code; limits on wrong attempts; secret keys for automatic jobs.
  • Regular backups, tested by restoring them, and monitoring for errors and misuse.

If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask to:

  • See the data held about you and who it was shared with.
  • Correct or update wrong or old details.
  • Delete your data, when it is no longer needed and the law allows.
  • Withdraw consent, as easily as you gave it. After withdrawal we stop using the data, but services that need it (for example, staying at the PG) may no longer be possible.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act.

Guests: please ask your PG owner first; they can correct most details in the app. You can also email us and we will pass it to your PG and help.

Owners and staff: email us from your login email.

We reply within 30 days.

8. Children

PG Manager is meant for adult guests (18 and over). If a guest is under 18, the PG owner must get consent from the guest's parent or guardian before adding them.
