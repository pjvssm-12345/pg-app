1. The service

PG Manager is a web app for running paying-guest accommodation: rooms and beds, guest joining, rent and deposits, receipts, meals, notices, repairs, reports, and (depending on your plan) profit reports, a public PG page with enquiries, Google review tools and an AI assistant. Guests can use their own links to see their stay, rent and meals.

New features may be added and some may change. We will tell you about important changes in the app. Features of each plan are listed in the app under What's in my plan.

3. Your guests' data (your responsibilities)

For your guests' personal data, you are the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and PG Manager is your Data Processor. You agree to:

  • Collect guest data only for running your PG, and only with the guest's consent (the joining form asks for it; when you add a guest yourself, explain and get their agreement).
  • Collect only what you need, and keep details correct and up to date.
  • Get and check a parent's or guardian's consent before approving any guest under 18.
  • Use the data only for your PG; do not sell it, share it for marketing, or misuse it.
  • Use enquiries from your public page only to reply to that enquiry; do not add them to marketing lists.
  • Share guest details with the police or authorities only when the law requires it.
  • Help your guests when they ask to see, correct or delete their data.

PG Manager will process guest data only to provide the service to you, keep it secure, use only trusted service providers, delete it as described in the Privacy Policy, and tell you without delay if a breach affects your data.

  • Reply to reviews politely and never share a guest's personal details in a reply.
  • PG Manager may hide a public page that is misleading or breaks the law.
